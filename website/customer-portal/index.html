Why a customer portal matters

Customers increasingly expect easy access to information. A portal can reduce repetitive questions and give your business a more professional way to keep customers updated.

Because JobPilot also keeps customer records, jobs, photos, quotes and invoices together, the portal is part of the wider workflow rather than another disconnected app.
