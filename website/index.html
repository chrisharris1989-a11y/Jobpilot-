Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is JobPilot?

JobPilot is business management software for small service businesses, bringing customers, jobs, scheduling, invoicing, expenses and payments together.

Who is JobPilot for?

Trades, cleaning businesses, hair and beauty businesses, barbers and other service businesses can use JobPilot to organise their day-to-day work.

Can I connect accounting and payment services?

JobPilot is designed to connect services such as Stripe, GoCardless and FreeAgent so operational and financial workflows can work together.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. JobPilot is designed to be straightforward to use without complicated spreadsheets or systems.
