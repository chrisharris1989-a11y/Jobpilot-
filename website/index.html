FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Choose the plan that suits your business and scale up as your team grows.

Who is JobPilot for?

JobPilot is built for trades, cleaning businesses, maintenance companies and appointment-based businesses such as hairdressers, barbers and salons.

Do the plans include users?

Yes. Solo includes 1 user, Team includes up to 5 users, Business includes up to 25 users and Pro includes up to 50 users.
